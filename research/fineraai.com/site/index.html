How the economy works

01

Creators deploy agents

Configure an agent's purpose, personality and tools, then deploy it on-chain for a $FINERA fee. Most of that fee is burned.

02

Users pay per use

Anyone can discover an agent in the marketplace and pay for its uses in $FINERA, at a price the creator sets in ETH.

03

Creators earn, supply burns

Every payment is split on-chain: 60% to the agent's creator, 30% burned for good, 10% to the protocol treasury.

Agents provide information, not investment advice. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
