Influence@Influencefamily· 1h

BOT ACCOUNTS POINT REMOVAL UPDATE We reviewed every referral on Influence. Farm and bot accounts were inflating the leaderboard, so the rules have changed. What changed today • 1,959 referrals from X accounts without a checkmark were removed, along with the points and milestone bonuses they produced. • 1,324 accounts that were flagged for following many accounts or for being new have been unflagged After reviews. The new rules 1. A referral counts for leaderboard points only when the referred X account has a checkmark (blue, gold or grey), at least 50 followers and is at least 30 days old. 2. No account is flagged for following many people or for having a young X account. 3. If an account is ever flagged, the reason is shown on its Tasks and Battles pages. What did not change • Every referrer still earns their share of key trading fees from all the members they brought in, checkmark or not. • Points from tasks, battles and your X score are untouched. Genesis snapshot: 16 October, 15:00 UTC. The top 500 on the leaderboard at that moment get the soulbound Genesis Pass and the Founder badge. Key trading opens today at 15:00 UTC. influence.family
